### About Osano:

Osano is an innovative B-Corporation built around a simple belief: privacy is a fundamental right. Our products help organizations operate confidently in a rapidly evolving regulatory and technological landscape.

### About the Role:

We’re looking for a **Senior AI Engineer** to join our Engineering team. This person will play a pivotal role in architecting and delivering privacy-first AI systems that power the next generation of Osano’s product capabilities.

This role goes beyond wrapping APIs or building chat interfaces. You will design and deploy autonomous AI agents, strict-source-following RAG systems, and evaluation frameworks that ensure reliability, compliance, and production-grade performance.

This position is ideal for someone who is highly technical, thrives in ambiguity, takes ownership of 0→1 initiatives, and believes responsible AI development is non-negotiable.

### Responsibilities:

#### *AI System Architecture*

* Architect and deploy autonomous AI agents and multi-agent workflows
* Design strict-source-following Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems that minimize hallucination and enforce citation integrity
* Implement guardrails, structured outputs, and validation layers

#### *Production Engineering*

* Build scalable backend services using FastAPI
* Orchestrate agentic workflows using LangGraph
* Deploy and manage AI workloads within AWS or Azure

*Optimize model performance, reliability, and cost*

#### *Evaluation & Continuous Improvement*

* Develop rigorous evaluation pipelines measuring:

+ Accuracy
+ Citation adherence
+ Latency
+ Reliability

* Establish metric-driven iteration cycles
* Continuously refine deployed systems based on measurable outcomes

#### *Privacy-First Development*

* Embed privacy-by-design principles across the AI stack
* Ensure compliance, data sovereignty, and secure handling practices
* Collaborate cross-functionally to align AI capabilities with regulatory standards
